How to Play

  • Aim: drag left/right anywhere on the course.
  • Swing: HOLD the swing button to build power, RELEASE to lock it, then TAP again to stop the accuracy needle in the middle.
  • Drink 🍺: the drunker you are the FURTHER you hit… but the world sways, aim wobbles and meters go crazy.
  • 🎯 Distance help: the gold tick on the power bar is the ideal power for this club (it accounts for lie and wind). The top bar suggests a club — tap it to switch. The drunker you are, the more the tick wobbles.
  • Beer steady · Whiskey big buzz · Vodka Soda crisp & steady · Jäger Bomb bullet-time meters · Tequila flips left/right · Keg Stand HOLD to chug · Absinthe fake holes & a hot tub · Rocket Fuel cart turbo · Moonshine monster power, blackout risk · Hot Sauce sobers you up (ouch) · Espresso/Water sober up · Mystery Shot chaos!
  • Hit 100% and you black out (+1 stroke). Sober up too fast after a bender and you get a hangover (weak shots).
  • Cart Dash: drive to the next tee through the checkpoints. Hit ramps for air, dodge ducks (honk 📯 to scare them off), cones and sprinklers. Gold, silver and bronze medals pay 🪙.
  • Liquid Courage: drive 200m+ while 50%+ drunk for bonus style points. Watch the wind, water, sand, trees, the windmill and the rogue cart!
